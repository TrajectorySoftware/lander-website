SampleRight

Run your environmental monitoring program from one live schedule that's clear and intuitive for the whole team. Know what's due, what got done, and why anything didn't.

Write the rule once

"Second Thursday of every month." "June and September only." Set the sampling requirement as a rule and SampleRight fills in the dates — for every station, for years.

Move work without breaking the plan

Weather, access, crews — field days slip. Drag a visit to another day, or shift the whole pattern going forward, and see exactly what moves before you commit.

Every skip has a reason

Missed a visit because of ice or a washed-out road? Record why, on the spot. When the regulator asks about the gap months later, the answer is already there.

The whole program at a glance

A calendar for planning the week, a timeline for the whole year. See what's covered and where the gaps are across every program and station.

Ready for the toolbox meeting

Assign the day's stations to crews and print a one-page day plan. Daily and weekly field summaries show what was planned against what was done.

Your data stays in Canada

Hosted in Montréal, with two-factor sign-in enforced for your whole organization. Clients and stakeholders get read-only access — they see the plan, nothing more.
